What to watch

September has slowed, and there is a straightforward reason

Direct orders are running 24% below August Through September 13 you are averaging $96.93 a day in direct online sales, against $127.42 in August. That is still 14% ahead of September last year, so the year-over-year gain holds, but the month-to-month drop is real and worth acting on.
Your ads have been switched off since August 4 They were paused at your request while we reviewed performance. On August 11 we applied five fixes inside the account: ads now point at your ordering page instead of your homepage, twenty negative keywords were added, the schedule was tightened to your trading hours, the display network was switched off, and targeting was narrowed to people physically nearby. All five are live and waiting. Turning the campaign back on is the only step left, and it costs nothing extra to do.

Method

What these numbers cover

Where the figures come from. The 2025 and early-2026 months are from your own Lightspeed U-Series order report, covering June 2025 through June 15, 2026. The Chowly months are from Chowly's order records. All figures are net sales: food and beverage before tax, and before delivery fees and tips.

What this does not measure. This report covers online ordering only. It does not include dine-in or counter sales, so it is not a picture of total restaurant revenue. Your Lightspeed sales reports through June are on file; sending us the July and August reports would let us show the online gain alongside the whole-restaurant number in the next review.

Partial months. June 2026 is excluded from the comparison because the old system was retired mid-month and Chowly went live on the 29th. September is reported as a daily average through the 13th rather than a monthly total.
