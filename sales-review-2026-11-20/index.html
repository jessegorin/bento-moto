What to watch

Two things worth a conversation

Uber Eats has dropped by about half Uber Eats went from roughly 32 orders a month over the summer to 18 in October and 15 so far in November. DoorDash and Grubhub both grew over the same stretch, so this looks specific to Uber Eats rather than a drop in demand. It is worth checking your store's visibility, hours and any promotions on that app.
Your Google ads have been running again since October 28 Through November 19 they have shown 14,207 times and sent 577 clicks to your ordering page. Over the same weeks, direct ordering is averaging $145 a day in November, its best month since you switched. Order-level ad tracking is not connected yet, so this report does not credit any specific sales to the ads.

Method

What these numbers cover

Where the figures come from. Every figure is from Chowly's order records and counts completed orders only. Sales are food and beverage before tax, tips and delivery fees.

Why July and August look slightly lower than last time. The September review counted every order record, including 7 orders that were resubmitted (and so appeared twice) and 6 that failed to go through. This review leaves those out, which lowers July and August by $433.78 in total. The same rule applies to every month here, so the comparison is like for like.

What this does not measure. This report covers online and marketplace orders only. It does not include dine-in or counter sales, so it is not a picture of total restaurant revenue. Seasonal patterns also differ between summer and fall, so part of any change may be the time of year rather than the channel.
